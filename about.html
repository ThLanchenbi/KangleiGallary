About Kanglei Gallery

Preserving and celebrating creative works

Welcome to Kanglei Gallery

Kanglei Gallery is a platform designed to showcase local talents, traditional art, modern musical compositions, creative poetry, short stories, videos, and published literary works.

Our platform operates as a community showcase where artists can submit their original items, and our admin team reviews and publishes them for the public.

How to Submit Your Content

Artists can send their art files or descriptions using either of the following platforms:

Telegram Channel

Send your artistic media directly to our Telegram channel or bot.
